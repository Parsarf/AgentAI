# Lumina — “Too many tabs”

Creative proposal · 27 October 2026 · 28-second vertical acquisition ad

## Strategy

Audience: solo founders, freelancers and independent professionals who research tools and make purchasing decisions themselves.

Promise: hand off a specific research task and inspect the sources and resulting file in one workspace.

Primary concept: start with a familiar mess of browser tabs, give Lumina a concrete comparison task, show actual browsing, then reveal the readable comparison and downloadable file. The result is the selling point. The brand enters by second three.

This is a production brief, not an executed demo or a claim of measured advertising performance. The October 26 system description supersedes older planning documents for understanding the current implementation; it still records production verification gaps. A successful recorded run is required before publishing the demonstration below. No paid offer or availability claim is assumed.

## Exact demo request

> Compare three booking tools for a solo consultant. Check their current monthly prices and free-plan limits on their official websites. Make a concise comparison, link your sources, and save a Markdown comparison file I can download. Flag anything you cannot verify. Do not sign up or buy anything.

Choose the three tools before recording and name them in the submitted prompt so the demonstration is reproducible. Show the generated file only after checking that its prices, plan limits, citations and download match the recorded run. Do not simulate task completion or invent source cards.

## Complete voiceover

“Still comparing everything in twelve tabs? Give Lumina the research. Ask it to compare your options, check prices, and find the limits. See what it found, with sources you can check. Then download the comparison and make your choice. Meet Lumina. Give it your next research task.”

About 50 words; allow the source and file reveals room to breathe.

## Visual and sound direction

- 1080 × 1920, 9:16. Recompose recordings for the vertical canvas: one relevant pane at a time, with large crops. Avoid shrinking the whole desktop into a phone frame.
- Record the actual customer-facing theme. For the character variant, use its parchment background, brown type and amber signal; for the Obsidian variant, retain charcoal and violet. Keep the ad consistent with the destination.
- Use the existing mascot briefly if it belongs to the recorded interface, alongside actual work. Never let it delay the demonstration or cover the result.
- Begin with recognizable cursor movement and a dry tab-click sound. At the Lumina reveal, simplify the sound to a quiet rhythmic bed. Keep narration clear; avoid trailer-style risers and constant transition effects.
- Add readable, high-contrast captions. Keep critical copy, sources and the domain away from placement controls; check each platform’s preview separately.
- Change the visual when the story moves to another step. Hold the comparison and downloaded file long enough to understand them. A real browser visiting a source is stronger evidence than an animated “Working” label.
- Use honest elapsed-time treatment. A 28-second edited video must not imply the whole task took 28 seconds. Mark accelerated footage; make no unmeasured time-saving claim.

## Hook variants to test

Keep the demo, body and CTA identical for the first test.

1. Problem: “Still comparing everything in twelve tabs?” Show the tab mess.
2. Demonstration: “I gave Lumina one research task. Here’s what came back.” Open with a glimpse of the actual comparison, then cut to the request.
3. Specific need: “Choosing a booking tool? Hand off the comparison.” Show the three named options and enter the request.

The second hook may work better when the finished result is visually strong. These are hypotheses, not established winners.

## Platform edits

- Instagram / Facebook Reels: use the master cut, a clear visual opening, and full captions. Check the interface overlays in the ad preview.
- YouTube Shorts: preserve the early product appearance, keep a clear spoken explanation, and hold the domain and next step at the end.
- TikTok: use a conversational delivery and direct screen recording; a creator face may introduce the task if it adds credibility. Do not add a fictional testimonial.

The story stays the same across placements; the opening delivery, caption placement and end-card layout are adjusted for each one.

## CTA and landing alignment

CTA on the proposed end card: “Explore Lumina.” Destination: getlumina.pro, subject to checking the live page before launch. The first destination screen should repeat the research-delegation benefit and show the same workflow.

If onboarding is invitation-only, use “Request access” only after a working request flow exists. Use “Try Lumina” only when a new visitor can actually start the advertised task. Do not advertise an app-store download, a free trial, price, unrestricted automation, guaranteed completion or private-data guarantees without current supporting evidence.

## Measurement

Compare hooks within each placement using comparable audiences and conditions. Use early retention and clicks to diagnose attention; choose the ad based on cost per new user who submits and successfully receives a meaningful task result. Paid conversion and retention become the downstream checks when those flows are active. Avoid selecting a winner solely on views or click-through rate.
