Lumina Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Lumina (“we”, “us”) is operated by Parsa Raouf. This policy covers the Lumina web app at getlumina.pro and the Lumina Browser extension for Chrome. Questions, requests or complaints: raouparsa@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your account and to do the tasks you ask for.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • The extension works only in tabs it opens itself, only while you have switched it on, and you can stop it at any moment.
  • If you choose to use your own model API key, your messages go to the company you chose instead of Anthropic.
  • Pictures and traces from browser tasks are deleted 24 hours after the task ends.
  • Everything else we hold about you stays until you ask us to delete it.

What we collect

This is everything we collect today. If that ever changes, we update this page first (see “Changes to this policy”).

Your account

  • Your email address.
  • Your password, kept only as a salted hash, so we cannot read it.
  • Your sign-in sessions, and a record of security-related actions on your account, such as signing in or pairing a browser.

Your content

  • The messages you send, the assistant’s replies, your tasks and their results, and activity records that show what the assistant did.
  • Logins and cards you choose to save are stored encrypted.
  • If you choose to use your own model, the address of the model service you entered, the model name, and your API key for it. The key is stored encrypted and is never shown again, apart from its last four characters.

Browser tasks

When Lumina does something in a web browser for you, we handle what you asked for, the addresses and titles of the pages it visits, a list of the controls on each page (buttons, links, fields), the page’s text, pictures of the page, the steps taken and the result. Password fields, and card fields that the page marks as card fields, are listed by type only; what is typed into them is not read as text. Pictures show what is on the screen, so they can show anything the page displays.

Searches and web pages

When the assistant searches the web or opens a web page for you, we handle your search terms and what the pages contain, and we keep a record of what it searched for and opened in your activity records. The assistant works in its own isolated environment, which can contact public websites while it works. A website the assistant opens receives the request like any other visitor would.

Technical data

Like any web service, our servers see your IP address and request details when you connect, and we keep ordinary operational logs to run and secure the service. These logs are kept for 7 days.

The Lumina Browser extension

The extension lets Lumina do a browsing task in a tab of your own Chrome instead of in Lumina’s own browser. You switch it on from the extension’s button, and you choose in Lumina (the browser icon at the top of every chat) whether your tasks use it.

What it can reach

  • Only tabs that it opens itself (and windows those tabs open). It groups them under the name “Lumina”. It does not read your other tabs, and it does not ask for permission to read all your websites.
  • While it is on, Lumina can use any site open in those tabs, including sites where you are signed in. We therefore suggest installing it in a separate Chrome profile that you use only for Lumina.

Why it asks for each permission

  • debugger: to read and act on the tabs it opened. Chrome shows “Lumina is debugging this browser” on those tabs while it does.
  • tabGroups: to group its tabs under “Lumina” so you can see which ones they are.
  • storage: to keep the settings listed below on your computer.

What it sends to us

Only while a task is running, over an encrypted connection to wss://backend.getlumina.pro/v1/device: the address and title of the page, up to 80 controls with their labels, up to 6,000 characters of the page’s text, and pictures of the page. The text typed into password fields, and into card fields that the page marks as card fields, is not sent. It talks to no other server.

What it keeps on your computer

A pairing token (a random secret that identifies this browser to Lumina), a pairing code until it has been used, whether the extension is switched on, and two settings that only developers use for testing. Uninstalling the extension removes them.

What it does not do

It contains no advertising, analytics or trackers, and it loads no code from the internet.

How to stop it

  • Use Turn off in the extension’s popup, or press Cancel on Chrome’s “is debugging this browser” bar. Either ends the session at once and closes its tabs.
  • Switch off the browser icon at the top of any chat.
  • Remove a browser in Lumina under Settings, Your browser. That cuts its connection and makes its token useless.

How we use your data

To run your account; to do the tasks you ask for; to keep the service secure and prevent abuse; and, if we start charging, to bill you. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who receives it

  • Anthropic provides the assistant’s model by default. It receives your messages and, during a browser task, the page content the assistant is asked to work with.
  • The company that runs the endpoint you enter, only if you choose to use your own model API key (Settings, Your own model). While it is on, it receives what Anthropic would otherwise receive: your messages, the assistant’s working notes, and the content of web pages and results the assistant reads for you. Its handling of that data is governed by its own terms, not this policy. Lumina does not charge you for that use and does not limit it by spend; you pay that company directly. The browser steps decided by TypeSafe (below) do not use your key.
  • TypeSafe provides the model that decides each step of a browser task. It receives a picture of the page and your task description. Before the text is sent we remove email addresses, phone numbers, card and ID numbers, street addresses, IP addresses and long keys from it. Pictures cannot be cleaned that way, so what is visible on a page is visible to them.
  • Brave Search receives the search terms when the assistant searches the web for you.
  • Two public DNS services, Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and Quad9 (9.9.9.9), turn website names into addresses whenever the assistant opens a page. They see the names of the sites it looks up, not what is on them.
  • Vercel serves getlumina.pro and passes your requests on to our server.
  • Our email provider sends sign-in and verification emails.
  • Our server hosting provider runs the machines the service lives on.
  • Stripe will handle payments if we start charging. We do not charge today. Stripe takes your card details directly, so we would not see your full card number. We will update this policy before we start.
  • We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Email raouparsa@gmail.com to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We will answer within 30 days.
  • You can archive chats yourself in the app.
  • You can switch off or remove the browser extension at any time, as described above.

Security

Connections to Lumina are encrypted. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, browser tokens only as hashes, and saved logins, cards and your own model key encrypted. Your model key is used only by our server to forward your requests; it is never placed inside your assistant’s environment. We accept only secure (https) addresses on the public internet for it, and we refuse addresses that point inside our own network. Each customer’s assistant runs in its own isolated environment. No service can promise perfect security, but we design for it and we fix problems when we find them.

Connecting other services and tools

Apart from choosing your own model (described above), you cannot connect other services or tools to Lumina today, such as email, calendars, files or outside tool servers. If we add that, we will tell you what we collect from each one before you connect it, and we will update this policy before the feature is available to you.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when what we collect, share or keep changes, and we change the date at the top when we do. A new feature that collects something new comes with an updated policy, not after it.
